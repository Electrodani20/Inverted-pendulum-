Unstable by nature. Balanced by feedback.

The cart moves beneath the pendulum to keep it upright. Phone tilt or the touch slider sets the target; a smooth position loop follows it, and an angle PID continually corrects the lean. Wind in Settings adds an independent disturbance. Motion is processed on your device and is never sent to a server. Turn the controller off to see why feedback matters.
